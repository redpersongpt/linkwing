Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 30, 2026

Short version: Linkwing doesn't collect, sell or share your personal data. Your study progress stays on your device.

What the app stores

Your answers, flashcard schedule, streaks, badges, exam date, daily goal and the optional name you enter are saved only on your device (in the app's local storage). There is no account and no sign-in. Nothing is uploaded to our servers.

What we don't do

We don't use analytics or advertising SDKs, we don't track you across apps or websites, and we don't collect contact details, location, contacts, photos or device identifiers.

Purchases

Linkwing Pro subscriptions are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details. Apple tells the app whether your subscription is active.

Notifications

If you turn on daily reminders, they are scheduled locally on your device. No push server is involved.

Contacting us

If you email us (for example through "Report a problem"), we receive only what you choose to send and use it solely to answer you and improve the questions.

Children

Linkwing is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

Deleting your data

Delete the app, or use You → Reset all progress, to erase everything it stored.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above.
