# Linkwing: Learn Networking

**Linkwing** is an iPhone app that teaches computer networking from zero and prepares you for the Cisco CCNA 200-301 exam. Kavi, a friendly blue cockatiel, guides you across an illustrated map from plain-language beginner lessons to exam-style questions, IOS command labs, subnetting drills and timed mock exams.

- **Website:** https://redpersongpt.github.io/linkwing/
- **App Store:** https://apps.apple.com/app/id6817419175
- **Platform:** iPhone, iOS 26 or later
- **Category:** Education
- **Price:** free to download; Linkwing Pro is an optional auto-renewing subscription
- **Developer:** Ata Tunc Bilge
- **Languages:** English, Spanish, Portuguese, Japanese, Korean, Arabic, Turkish, Simplified Chinese, with an in-app switch. The interface and the 17 Start Here beginner lessons (including diagram captions and a 60-term tap-to-explain glossary) are fully translated into all 8 languages. Exam-level lessons show translated titles and summaries with the full text in English, except Japanese, where they are fully translated. Exam-style questions and IOS labs are in English (the CCNA exam is offered in English and Japanese).
- **Offline:** yes, and no account is needed

## What is inside

- 17 beginner lessons in 2 "Start Here" worlds (what a network is, switches, routers, IP and MAC addresses, Wi-Fi, VLANs and more), with tap-to-explain words
- A 5-question adaptive check-up that skips what you already know
- 15 exam worlds on an illustrated map: Network Foundations, Ethernet, IPv4 subnetting, Switching, VLANs, Spanning Tree, Routing, OSPF, IPv6, Services, Security, Wireless, Automation, Troubleshooting, Exam Summit
- 1,136 original exam-style questions (exhibits, choose-two, ordering, matching, calculations)
- 929 flashcards with spaced repetition
- 75 IOS command labs where you type real commands
- Subnetting Gym with unlimited generated drills
- Timed mock exams of 20, 50 and 100 questions, weighted like the blueprint
- An honest predicted score out of 1000, shown only after 40 first-try answers, with an uncertainty range, pass-chance estimate, a 6-point "Road to exam day" checklist, a trend chart and an exam-day forecast
- CCNA 200-301 v1.1 (exams until 2 February 2027) and v2.0 (from 3 February 2027); the blueprint is picked from your exam date
