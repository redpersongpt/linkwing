FAQ

Which CCNA does Linkwing cover?
Both CCNA 200-301 v1.1 (until Feb 2, 2027) and the new v2.0 (from Feb 3, 2027). Your exam date picks the right blueprint; you can change it in You → Edit profile.

How is "Will I pass?" calculated?
From your first-try answers per topic, weighted by Cisco's section percentages and blended with your mock exams. Repeated questions count less, so memorising can't inflate it.

How do I restore Linkwing Pro?
Open the Pro screen and tap Restore. Manage or cancel your subscription in your Apple Account settings.

Does it work offline?
Yes. Everything, including lessons, questions, labs and mocks, works without internet.

How do I reset my progress?
You → Reset all progress.
